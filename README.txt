A LITTLE SCRAPBOOK FOR YOU
==========================

FILES
- index.html: page content and all the words she will read
- styles.css: colors, layout, scrapbook styling, and mobile responsiveness
- script.js: the floating hearts, reveal effects, letter toggle, and answer buttons
- images/: the four photos included in the original template

HOW TO OPEN
1. Keep the files in this same folder so the relative links keep working.
2. Double-click index.html to preview it in a browser.
3. An internet connection is needed for the optional Google Fonts. The page still works with fallback fonts offline.

PERSONALIZE BEFORE SHARING
1. In index.html, replace [her name] in the letter greeting with her name.
2. Replace [your name] in the sign-off with your name.
3. Edit the appreciation-card text and photo captions so they mention real things you appreciate about her.
4. Replace images/memory-1.jpg through images/memory-4.jpg with your chosen photos. Keep the exact filenames, or update each img src in index.html.
5. You can change the page title by editing the <title> text and the main <h1>.

ABOUT THE ANSWER BUTTONS
The two buttons at the bottom only show a response on the page. They do not send you a notification, store her answer, or submit anything to a server. If you want to know her response, view the page together or ask her directly afterward.

LANGUAGE AND TONE
The copy is written in simple English with a little natural Taglish. Feel free to adjust it so it sounds like you, not like a generic letter.
