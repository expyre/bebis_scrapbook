I want more conversations that last longer than we intended, more moments where we make each other laugh, more random conversations about absolutely nothing, and more opportunities to learn things about you that I haven't discovered yet. I catch myself looking forward to hearing from you, wondering how you've been, and wanting to tell you about mine. And I hope that, in whatever way I can, I get to remind you that you're someone worth knowing, appreciating, loving and caring for. Thank you for coming into my life, for letting me get close to you, and for giving me so many moments that I genuinely cherish. From your one and only,
